About

I keep watch on the bridge of oil tankers routing through Japan, South Korea, Singapore, Hong Kong, and PNG — navigation, bond store, onboard compliance. Off watch, I write code: mostly React and Python, increasingly React Native.

Most of what I build starts as a problem on a real ship — a warning I couldn't find fast enough, a log I was reconciling by hand — and ends up as something anyone can use.
